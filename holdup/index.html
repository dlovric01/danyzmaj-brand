Hold Up

A sign you hold up. Fullscreen text, sized to fit, readable across a room.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas — write to danijel@danyzmaj.com. Include your device and iOS version if something misbehaves.

How it works

Type straight onto the sign; the text sizes itself to the largest size that fits. Pick one of six backgrounds — the text colour is derived automatically, so the sign is always readable. Save signs as presets and bring them back in two taps. The screen stays awake at full brightness while a sign is showing.

Privacy

Hold Up collects nothing. No network, no analytics, no accounts — read the privacy policy.
