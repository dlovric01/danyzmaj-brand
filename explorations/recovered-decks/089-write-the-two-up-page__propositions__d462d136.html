DANYZMAJ · THE SHORTLIST · TWO MARKS

The two that
made the cut.

Round two is gone. These are the two you picked: the fanged Z and the twohead. Nothing else is on the table, and nothing here is new — they are exactly as they were.

They are drawn on the same Z skeleton, to the same weight, in the same palette and the same typeface. That is not a coincidence of taste — it is measurable, and it is the reason the next section is worth reading before you choose.

SHARED SKELETON
Bar 26 · diagonal 24 · same terminals. Identical letterform underneath.

SHARED PALETTE
Chalk #FAF7F2 · jet #121212 · signal #E5231B.

SHARED TYPE
Chivo 900 for the wordmark, Chivo for everything else.

08

Fanged Z

one head · the worker
48 · 32 · 16
DANYZMAJ

A heavy Z opens a jaw at its top-left terminal, grows a brow horn and a barbed tail, and sticks its tongue out. You read the letter first and the animal half a second later.

BEST FOR
Everything, every day. Favicon, avatar, invoice header, lockup, sticker. It is calm enough to sit next to text and still legible at 16 pixels.
RISK
On its own it is the more ordinary of the two. It has no fact about itself to tell.
08C

Twohead

two heads · the seal
48 · 32 · 16
DANYZMAJ

A jaw at each terminal, because a Z already has 180-degree rotational symmetry and the Slavic zmaj is rarely single-headed. Turn it upside down and it is the same drawing.

BEST FOR
The moment that needs weight: a stamp on finished work, the reverse of a card, a case-study cover, the one sticker you actually hand someone.
RISK
Twice the detail, so it is the weaker of the two at 16 pixels and busier in a tight lockup.

THE THING WORTH NOTICING

You do not have to choose.

Because both are cut from the same skeleton, they are not competing logos — they are one identity with two registers, the way a publisher has a logo and a colophon. Pick which one does the everyday work and which one is reserved, and you get the calm of 08 plus the story of 08C without diluting either.

08 Fanged Z — the working mark

Used constantly, without ceremony. Roughly 95% of appearances.

  • Favicon, app icon, avatar
  • Site header and email signature
  • Invoices, proposals, contracts
  • Anything below 32 pixels

08C Twohead — the seal

Used rarely and on purpose, so it keeps its weight. Never below 48 pixels.

  • Sign-off on delivered work
  • Reverse of a card, cover of a case study
  • Stickers, packaging, anything physical
  • The mark you explain out loud
