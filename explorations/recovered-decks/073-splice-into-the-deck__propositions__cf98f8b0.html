DANYZMAJ · BRAND DIRECTIONS · NINE PROPOSITIONS

Nine ways to be
a dragon.

Zmaj means dragon in Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian and Slovenian. A name with an animal inside it is rare, so every direction below spends the identity on that animal — but each one comes from a different world, and they lead to very different companies.

Each is drawn for real: the mark, three sizes, the palette with hex values, the type pairing, and one thing it makes. Nothing here is a mood board.

01–06 lead with the animal. 07–09 lead with the letter Z and let the dragon arrive second — one breathing fire, one that is the creature, one that is a character. Those three are the playful end of the range.

CONSTANT ACROSS ALL NINE
The name, the domain, and the dragon.

WHAT DECIDES IT
Who has to trust you in the first four seconds.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
Pick one. It gets built out to the depth 01 already is.

HOW TO CHOOSE

Not by which is prettiest.

Ask what the buyer needs to believe. 02 and 04 sell engineering rigour. 01 and 03 sell judgement and permanence. 05 sells contemporary confidence. 06 sells a person you would like working with. 07, 08 and 09 sell energy — they are the ones people repeat, sticker and screenshot, and the ones that will look youngest in five years.

If the letter Z is the thing you keep coming back to, the shortlist is 08 for a mark that has to work everywhere from a favicon to a hoodie, 07 if you want the loudest version of that, and 09 if you want something with a face.

Direction 01 is already a complete kit — logo cuts, tokens, favicons, social card, guidelines — in this same folder at index.html. Any of the other five can be taken to that depth. Say the number.
