DANYZMAJ · ROUND TWO · SIX CONSTRUCTIONS

Six more ways
to draw the Z.

These are not variations on 08 — each one is built by a different method. One is swiped, one is bitten, one is written with a pen, one is cut for a stencil, one is articulated into segments, one is squeezed tall. The dragon arrives differently in each.

Type, palette and layout are held identical across all six on purpose, so the only thing you are comparing is the letter itself. Whichever you point at gets its own type and colour afterwards.

Where you were

Direction 08, the fanged Z, for comparison. Everything below is a different way of getting to the same place.

Chisel

HOW THESE DIFFER FROM 08

08 added a face. These change the handwriting.

10 Claw and 11 Bite are the two that a stranger will decode fastest — one gesture each. 12 Chisel is the only one that will not look like software. 13 Stencil and 14 Segment both hand you a system beyond the logo. 15 Tall is the closest to 08 and the most aggressively a dragon.
