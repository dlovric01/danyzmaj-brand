FAMILY ONE · IT BREATHES

Three ways to set the Z alight.

Same soot ground, same flame orange and ember gold. What changes is where the fire comes from: beside the letter, out of a mouth, or off the letter's own edge.

07

Firebreather

The letter stays a pure slab Z. The plume erupts off its top-left terminal — no head, no eye. The fire does all the talking.

46·28·16
BEST FOR
The cleanest of the three. Loud on a dark site, a sticker or a badge, and the letter never stops being readable.
RISK
Two colours by nature, so single-ink printing loses the fire entirely.
07B

Forge

NEW

The fanged Z from 08, breathing. Jaw, fang, eye and brow horn are all still there — the plume now comes out of the mouth.

46·28·16
BEST FOR
Anyone who likes 07 and 08 both. It is the only mark here that is a letter, a dragon and a fire at once.
RISK
Busiest at the head end, and it depends on the fire being visible, so it can never go single-ink.
07C

Blaze

NEW

No plume. The fire sits on the letter itself, licking off the top edge of every stroke. The Z is not breathing fire — it is on fire.

46·28·16
BEST FOR
The most graphic of the three. Superb as a large header, a hoodie back or a poster.
RISK
The tallest silhouette here, so it fits square containers worst and crops badly as an avatar.

FAMILY TWO · IT BITES

The two you kept.

Both drawn on the same Z skeleton, in one ink plus one red. On the dark ground the red is warmed to #FF4A3D — the original #E5231B only measures 4.28:1 against soot, which is too low to sit in a dark interface.

08

Fanged Z

A heavy Z opens a jaw at its top-left terminal, grows a brow horn and a barbed tail, and sticks its tongue out.

46·28·16
BEST FOR
Everything, every day: favicon, avatar, invoice, lockup. Calm enough to sit beside text and legible at 16px.
RISK
On its own it is the more ordinary of the pair. It has no fact about itself to tell.
08C

Twohead

A jaw at each terminal, because a Z already has 180-degree rotational symmetry and the Slavic zmaj is rarely single-headed.

46·28·16
BEST FOR
The moment that needs weight: a stamp on finished work, the reverse of a card, the one sticker you hand someone.
RISK
Twice the detail, so it is weaker at 16px and busier in a tight lockup.

YOU ASKED FOR THIS ONE

08 built out dark.

The fanged Z as an actual dark identity rather than a mark on a black square: soot ground, chalk letter, one hot red doing the work of every accent. Nothing else added.

THE THING WORTH NOTICING

07B is not a third option. It is your two favourites added together.

+ =

You liked the fanged Z and you liked the firebreather. Forge is the same jaw, the same brow horn, the same barbed tail — with the plume moved from beside the letter to out of the mouth, which is where a dragon actually keeps it.

If it wins, 08 stays in the system as the quiet cut for 16px and for single-ink printing, where the fire could not survive anyway. You would be choosing a pair, not replacing one with the other.
