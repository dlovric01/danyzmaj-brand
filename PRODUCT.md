# danyzmaj — product truth

## What it is
A one-person company trading as **danyzmaj**, at **danyzmaj.com**. One operator does the work,
signs it, and is the only person a client ever deals with.

## Name
`dany` + `zmaj`. **Zmaj** is *dragon* in Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian and Slovenian. Confirmed by the
owner as the intended meaning, with a decision to lean into it rather than abstract it away. The name
carries an animal, which is rare and worth spending the whole identity on.

## Audience
Client-side buyers, currently one in the United States, with more expected. They are evaluating a
single contractor against agencies and against other solo operators. What they need to believe:
this person has craft, taste, and can be trusted with load-bearing work without supervision.

## Mechanism
One senior operator, direct access, no account layer, no handoffs. The identity's job is to make
"one person" read as *concentrated*, not as *small*.

## Standing brand commitments
- The dragon is literal, not abstract. It is drawn, not implied by a monogram.
- The mark doubles as a **Z** — the letter of `zmaj`. It is a creature and a letter at once.
- Cyrillic (`ЗМАЈ`) is **not** part of the system: the chosen display face has no Cyrillic coverage,
  and the audience is US-facing. The Balkan origin lives in the name and the copy, not in a second script.

## Open — needs the owner's answer
1. **Discipline.** The identity assumes technical/software contract work, inferred from the working
   environment, not stated. Nothing in the visual system depends on it; only the descriptor copy does.
2. **Tagline.** Three candidates are parked in the guide colophon. None is committed.
3. **Founding year**, legal entity name, and any registration line for the imprint. Not invented.
