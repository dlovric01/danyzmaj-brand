Misuse

The dragon faces left, sits flat, and is one solid colour. Everything below breaks it.

Mirrored. It always faces left.

Rotated. Only the seal is struck at an angle, and only in motion.

Stretched. Scale proportionally or not at all.

Recoloured. Cinnabar, ink or bone.

Gradient fill. The mark is flat ink.

Shadowed. The strike shadow belongs to the seal, never to the bare mark.

Plate 04

Colour

One saturated colour carries the surface. Cinnabar is stamp ink: it belongs to marks and display type. Every ratio below is measured, not estimated.

ink#171210ground · the press bed
cinnabar#DA3A20marks + display only · 4.08:1 on ink
oxide#8E1F0Etext-safe red · 7.66:1 with bone
brass#C08A2Ehairlines + labels · 6.12:1 on ink
bone#F2EDE4paper + primary text · 15.93:1 on ink
ash#948881contrast floor · 5.39:1 on ink

The one rule

Cinnabar measures 4.08:1 on ink and 3.91:1 on bone. Both fail the 4.5:1 floor for body copy, in both directions. So cinnabar never sets small text — that is what oxide is for. This is also why the primary button below is oxide, not cinnabar.

Body copy on cinnabar. 4.08:1. Not allowed.

Measured failure, shown so it is not repeated.

Oxide carries text.

A field that has to hold a sentence uses oxide, where bone measures 7.66:1. Secondary text on a coloured field is tinted from that hue — never grey.

Plate 05

Type

Bodoni cut his own punches and printed his own books — one hand, start to finish. That is the reason for the display face, and the hairline is what makes cinnabar look struck rather than printed.

Zmaj

Bodoni Moda · 700 · opsz 96 · tracking −0.022em

Display only. Never below 28px — the hairlines break. Fallback: Didot, Bodoni 72.

Archivo carries every running word: interface labels, proposals, invoices, the long paragraph on a case page. It holds at 13px, stays out of the way, and keeps its shape next to a Didone without pretending to be one.

Plate 06

System

Nothing is rounded except the seal. Borders are brass hairlines. Depth is a strike: a hard 2px offset, no halo. Controls press down when you use them.

HairlineStruck

Actions · hover, press, disabled

Primary is oxide so a 13px label clears 4.5:1. Press = 2px down, shadow gone.

Missing the domain ending — try .com.

Fields · rest, focus, error
